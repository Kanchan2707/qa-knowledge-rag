# QA Onboarding

## First week
A new QA engineer gets a mentor on day one. Local environment setup should be finished within 2 days. During the first week the new engineer shadows the on-call rotation and files their first bug report by day 5.

## 30/60/90 plan
By day 30 the engineer owns a small test suite. By day 60 they write a test plan for a feature. By day 90 they join the on-call rotation as a secondary.
