# QA On-Call

## Rotation
The QA on-call rotation changes weekly on Monday at 9 AM Eastern. Each week has a primary and a secondary engineer. The secondary takes over if the primary does not respond.

## Response times and handoff
A page must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. At the start of each rotation the outgoing engineer writes a handoff document that lists open incidents, flaky tests and anything unusual from the week.
